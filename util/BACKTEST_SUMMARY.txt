CONCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STRESS TEST VERDICT: The rebalancing strategy underperformed during this
extreme market stress event. The higher equity allocation (67% vs 60%) and
factor tilts (value, small-cap) that typically provide long-term benefits
became liabilities during the crisis.

DIVERSIFICATION BENEFIT: Despite underperformance, diversification across
11 asset classes helped contain losses to under 10% during one of the fastest
market crashes in history.

RISK-ADJUSTED VIEW: The Sharpe ratio of -0.757 indicates the strategy took
more risk without commensurate returns during this period. The strategy's risk
profile is appropriate for investors willing to accept higher volatility.

LONG-TERM PERSPECTIVE: This crisis period alone should not determine the
strategy's merit. Value and small-cap tilts typically outperform over full
market cycles. The April 2020 rebound (+12.7%) suggests the strategy would
have participated in the subsequent recovery.

REBALANCING DISCIPLINE: The monthly rebalancing forced the strategy to "buy
the dip" in March 2020, which is precisely what disciplined investors should
do. This mechanical approach removes emotional decision-making.
